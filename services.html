Services

Designed, printed and installed in-house

EXO looks after signage of every scale — from corflute and decals through to fleet wraps, illuminated letters and large floor graphics.

On the road

Vehicle wraps

Full and partial wraps that turn everyday vehicles into moving billboards. We design, print and install in-house, and can remove old film cleanly when it is time for a change.

  • Full van, ute and car wraps
  • Partial wraps, door signs and cut graphics
  • Perforated window film where needed
  • Professional removals and refreshes

One look, every vehicle

Fleet graphics

Keep a whole fleet consistent, whether you are branding one ute or rolling out a set. We also supply reflective safety and conspicuity markings to help vehicles stay visible day and night.

  • Matched branding across cars, utes and vans
  • High-visibility reflective tapes and chevron livery
  • Markings specified for Australian road use
  • Custom fit, install and ongoing support

Make the front door count

Shopfront & building signage

Bold, weather-resistant signs for storefronts, offices and sites. We also produce lightweight corflute and temporary signs for events, real estate and construction, and work with national retail brands as well as local independents.

  • Shopfront and outdoor building signs
  • Office and reception signs in acrylic, metal or frosted glass
  • Corflute and temporary site signage
  • Computer-cut lettering and panels

Seen after dark

Illuminated & 3D signs

Lightbox signs, LED displays and raised lettering for premium presence on the street. We help you choose the right build for the facade, the viewing distance and the hours you need to be noticed.

  • 3D built-up and halo-lit lettering
  • Lightbox and LED signage
  • Fascia and building-mounted displays

Inside the space

Floor & window graphics

High-impact floor decals for retail, workplaces and sporting venues, plus frosted film, window signs and decorative graphics that still let the light in.

  • Durable floor graphics and wayfinding
  • Frosted window film and privacy graphics
  • Retail window displays and opening-hours signs
  • Large-format floor work for venues

Print that travels

Banners & print

Portable and promotional print to back up your fixed signage — plus the finishing touches that keep a brand consistent.

  • Banners, wallpaper and A-frames
  • Stickers, decals, labels and custom prints
  • Business cards and short-run promotional print

From concept to the wall

Design & project support

Need a fresh layout, a brand-consistent wrap, or help managing a larger install? We can join a job at any stage — from graphic design and corporate identity through to site surveys, project management and on-site liaison.

  • Graphic design and corporate identity
  • Site surveys and project management
  • On-site installation and brand support
