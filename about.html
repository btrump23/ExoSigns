About

The studio behind the signs

EXO Signs & Graphics Co. is a Greensborough workshop with deep roots in screenprinting, automotive graphics and film application.

Who we are

EXO was founded by Craig Farquharson, who started in the sign trade as a screenprinter in the early 1980s and has spent more than forty years across signage, automotive decals, digital print and specialist film application.

After years installing and project-managing for larger sign and digital companies, Craig built EXO as a hands-on local studio — small enough to stay personal, experienced enough to take on demanding wraps, building signs and multi-site work.

Today the team designs, prints and installs from Unit 11, 10-14 Simms Road, Greensborough, helping businesses across Melbourne’s north-east be seen. Jobs of every size are welcome, from a set of labels or a door sign through to a fleet rollout or a fully managed install.

We are factory-trained specialist film installers and can join a project at any stage: design and supply, installation only, or site surveys and project management.
