What we do

Signage that works as hard as you do

From a single ute door to a full fleet wrap or a lit shopfront, EXO looks after design, print, install and the details in between.

Vehicle wraps

Full and partial wraps that turn vans, utes and cars into moving billboards, finished in-house.

Fleet graphics

Consistent branding across one vehicle or many, plus reflective safety and conspicuity markings.

Shopfront & building signage

Durable outdoor signs for storefronts, offices and sites, built to weather Australian conditions.

Illuminated & 3D signs

Lightbox signs, LED displays and raised lettering that keep your brand visible after dark.

Floor & window graphics

Floor decals, frosted film and window graphics for retail, workplaces and large venues.

Banners, print & design

Banners, A-frames, corflute, stickers, labels and original graphic design to complete the job.
